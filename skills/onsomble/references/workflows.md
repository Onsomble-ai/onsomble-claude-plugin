# Workflows

A few recipes covering most requests. Steps name the tools in call order; skip a step only when the user already gave you its output.

## Visibility audit

For "how are we doing in AI search" and periodic reviews.

1. `list_sites_and_topics` — resolve the Site (ask if ambiguous).
2. `get_visibility_overview` — headline metrics and deltas vs the previous Scan.
3. `get_visibility_timeline` (metric `visibility`, then `sentiment` if tone moved) — confirm whether the delta is a trend or a blip.
4. For the weakest headline metric, drill down: low Visibility → `get_prompt_results` on the prompts that missed the brand; weak Sentiment → `get_narratives` for the stories and quoted evidence behind the tone; strong competitors in the overview → competitor review below.
5. `get_references` — which domains AI cites in this market, and whether the brand's own pages are among them. Follow its domain pages; use `get_reference_domain` for full source URLs and usage history under the same filters and scopeToken.
6. `get_recommendations` — close with the top prioritised action, not a generic suggestion.

Report shape: headline + delta first, evidence-attributed findings, one prioritised action. See `../examples/sample-analysis.md`.

For performance reads, follow overview competitor pages and every scorecard/timeline `nextCursor` with unchanged filters. History returns 20 flat metric records per page and a page is a partial history. Read all matching observations before claiming a complete trend. Topic platform/region timelines require an explicit entity ID; model comparison can use the Scan's saved focal entity, with explicit selection for neutral Topics. Saved Topic context describes the Scan rather than today's configuration.

## Competitor review

For "who is beating us and why".

1. `list_sites_and_topics`, then `get_visibility_overview` — note the strongest competitor the summary names.
2. `list_competitors` — the tracked competitors and their `competitorId`s; `list_discovered_competitors` for brands AI mentions that are not tracked yet (filter by `kind`).
3. `get_visibility_overview` or `get_visibility_timeline` with `competitorId` (tracked only) for the one or two that matter — their metrics beside the brand, or their trend over time.
4. `get_prompt_results` filtered to the battleground — read what AI actually says when it prefers the competitor.
5. `get_references` — check whether the competitor's cited domains explain their advantage.

Discovered-but-untracked brands with high signal are worth surfacing: the user may want to track them, which changes Share of Voice comparability from that point on.

## Set up the account

For a brand-new account, or "make us an agency", "update our account details".

1. `get_account` — the account's name, type (business or agency), and profile (website, description, services, default industry and country).
2. `update_account` — change any of those; pass only the fields you want to change. Set `type` to `agency` to manage several client brands, or `business` for a single brand. **Switching the type is only allowed while the account is empty** — no Clients and no Sites — so do it before setting anything else up. Existing free tiers move to the matching free tier; paid tiers are preserved. Omitted fields remain unchanged; explicit null clears websiteUrl, description, defaultIndustry or defaultCountry, and services:[] clears the services list. name/type/services cannot be null. licenseTier and billingCycle are returned by get_account but cannot be edited here. After an uncertain write, read get_account before repeating it.

A business tracks its own brands directly; an agency groups them under Clients (`create_client` and `update_client`).

## Manage Clients (agency accounts)

1. `list_clients` — discover accessible Clients, including those with no Sites. It defaults to 25 items (maximum 100); follow every non-null `nextCursor`, including after an empty page. The Client preview in `list_sites_and_topics` includes only its first 20 Clients; subject pagination does not complete Client discovery.
2. `get_client(clientId)` — read saved name, website, industry, description and `knownBrands` before editing. Use `list_sites_and_topics(clientId)` for the Client's actual Sites and topics.
3. `create_client` — save a new Client with a nonblank name and optional details. Creation checks agency eligibility and Client allowance. Every successful call creates a new identity; after an uncertain result, inspect the inventory and details before retrying.
4. `update_client` — supply `clientId` and at least one editable field. Omission preserves fields; `null` clears website, industry or description. Name cannot be cleared. `knownBrands` replaces the full descriptive list; `null` or `[]` clears it. These brand facts do not create Sites or associations.
5. `archive_client({clientId})` — archives the Client and every Site and Topic under it. They leave `list_clients` and `list_sites_and_topics`, their scan schedules are paused, and every later write is refused with `client_archived` or `site_archived`; `get_client` and the report tools keep reading them. Refused with `scan_running` while any of those Sites has a scan in progress. Read `get_client` and all pages of `list_sites_and_topics(clientId)` first; explain that every linked Site and Topic will be archived and obtain explicit user confirmation. There is no restore through MCP. The result includes `alreadyArchived` and `archivedSites`, the combined number of Sites and Topics newly archived by this call (not total inventory). After an uncertain result, read `get_client(clientId)` and check `client.archivedAt` before deciding whether to retry.

Creation requires Account access. Existing credentials restricted to a Client can read/edit only that Client. These tools launch no Scan and expose no Client deletion; archive is the retirement step.

## Set up a new Site

For "add a site for acme.com", "start tracking this brand", "create a site".

1. Research the website using the AI assistant's own research capabilities and review the business profile with the user. On an agency account, resolve the Client and its `clientId` first.
2. `create_site` with `url`, the reviewed `businessProfile`, and `clientId` where required. The profile includes businessName, industry, market, description, domain and a products/services list. Optional fields include operatingRegions, aliases and `strategicGoal: {objective}` for the initial strategy note. Creation retries preserve a note already saved; use `update_site` to replace it or clear with `strategicGoal: null`. There is no Onsomble research or separate confirmation tool.
3. Creation completes setup with default models and no prompts; it does not launch a Scan. An existing active URL returns `site_already_exists`; inspect the inventory and use `get_site` rather than retrying blindly. If a prior attempt was interrupted, the same URL can reuse its existing setup record. The `siteId` form can complete profile-ready setup or read back an already-active Site; it preserves the active profile. A failed ID must be retried using its original URL and reviewed profile. Use only an identity returned by Onsomble.
4. `get_site` to inspect saved details; `update_site` for edits. Omission preserves fields. Profile `products` and `operatingRegions` replace their lists; `null` or `[]` clears them. Alias groups use add/remove; `null` clears a group. Profile products describe offerings, distinct from `get_scan_config.products` strategy definitions. Required profile text cannot be cleared. The primary URL/domain and Client reassignment are not editable here.
5. Configure the Scan with the user — audiences, strategy products and journey stages, then questions, models and regions. Read `get_scan_config` first. A Scan cannot run until the Site has prompts.
6. Show the complete saved setup and explain Scan allowance use. You must explicitly confirm with the user before calling `trigger_scan`. Configure a schedule only if requested.

Both `get_site` and `get_topic` include the saved `strategicGoal` or null. For Topic details use `get_topic(topicId)`; create/edit research with `create_topic`/`update_topic`. Both accept `strategicGoal`; ID creation recovery can save an absent initial note but preserves a saved note, while update replaces it or null clears it. Omission preserves the note. Shared configuration/report tools still use its ID as `siteId`. There is no MCP tool to delete a Site or Client; retire one with archive instead.

## Archive a Site or Topic

For "archive this site", "stop tracking this brand", "retire this topic".

`archive_site({siteId})` or `archive_topic({topicId})`. The subject leaves `list_sites_and_topics`, its scan schedule is paused, and every later write (`trigger_scan`, `update_scan_schedule`, prompt, competitor, strategy and recommendation writes, further edits) is refused with `site_archived`. `get_site`, `get_topic` and every report tool keep reading it. Refused with `scan_running` while a scan is in progress; wait for the scan to finish. Every Site and Topic read carries `archivedAt` (null while active). There is no restore through MCP, so obtain explicit user confirmation before archiving. Read the saved subject first and use its matching archive tool; Site and Topic IDs cannot be interchanged. The result retains the subject in `site` and includes `alreadyArchived`. After an uncertain result, read `get_site` or `get_topic` by the retained ID and check `archivedAt` before deciding whether to retry. None of these archive tools starts a Scan or spends Scan allowance. Ordinary update tools accept editable fields only; they reject `operation: "archive"`.

## Manage portfolios (agency accounts)

For "group these clients into a portfolio", "make a portfolio for our AU brands", "add this client to the portfolio".

A Portfolio is an agency Account collection of Clients plus directly attached Sites and topics. A member Client includes all its current/future brand Sites; its topics require explicit attachment.

1. `list_portfolios` — find saved IDs, names, descriptions and Client/Site/topic counts. Optional search matches name/description. This uses numbered pages of 50 Portfolios, not a cursor; follow returned `page` through `pageCount`.
2. `get_portfolio(portfolioId)` — read saved name/description and its contents before editing. Contents use their own numbered pages of 50 top-level entries. Member Clients include their full child lists; directly attached Sites/topics appear separately when their Client is not a member. A Site's `direct` marker identifies its independent attachment.
3. `create_portfolio` — save a nonblank name, optional description and initial `clientIds`, `siteIds` and/or `topicIds`. Supply 1–100 IDs total across the lists. Resolve Client IDs via `list_clients` and Site/topic IDs via `list_sites_and_topics`. Names are unique within the Account ignoring case/surrounding spaces; after an uncertain creation inspect the inventory/details before retrying.
4. `update_portfolio` — supply `portfolioId` and name and/or description. Omission preserves the field; `description: null` clears it. Blank descriptions also normalize to null. Name cannot be cleared. Membership is changed separately.
5. `add_members_to_portfolio` / `remove_members_from_portfolio` — supply `portfolioId` and 1–100 distinct IDs total across the three typed lists. One call can include Clients, Sites and topics. Wrong kinds, duplicates or foreign Account IDs are refused. Re-adding an existing membership or removing an absent membership leaves it in the requested state.

Removing direct Site membership may leave it included through a member Client. Removing a Client may leave independently attached Sites/topics. These operations never delete the underlying objects or their scan history; removing the last member leaves an empty Portfolio. Use `get_portfolio` to inspect resultant direct/inherited inclusion after edits or an uncertain result. Account access is required; Client-restricted credentials cannot use Portfolio operations. There is no MCP Portfolio deletion and these tools start no Scan.

## Configure a Site

For "add this prompt", "track this competitor", "turn off Gemini", "scan weekly".

For a new scan, establish the objective and audiences with the user before completing the setup. Use information already provided and ask about missing details. Discuss which classifications are useful, draft questions that cover the objective and audiences, and review the proposed questions, assignments, tracked entities, regions and models together. Optional classification fields can be deliberately omitted where appropriate.

1. `get_scan_config` without `scanId` — read the working setup first. Its definitions (personas, products, journey stages, models) supply the IDs used for edits. When discussing scan strategy, ask for the overarching objective if `strategicGoal` is absent; save it with `update_site`/`update_topic` as `strategicGoal: {objective}`, or clear with `strategicGoal: null`. `get_scan_goal_history` shows saved edits. This does not start a Scan or generate questions.
2. Use the independent definition tools: `create_persona`, `update_persona`, `remove_persona`; `create_product`, `update_product`, `remove_product`; `create_journey_stage`, `update_journey_stage`, `remove_journey_stage`. Create takes name and optional description/aliases; update/remove use itemId from `get_scan_config`. Journey stages accept name/description, including custom stages; canonical UI/generation policies may still use registry details. Omitted fields preserve, description:null clears, aliases:null clears to []. Names must be nonblank and unique. Updates preserve IDs and refresh active stable-ID labels; removal cleans active assignments. Questions use `create_prompt`, `update_prompt`, `retire_prompt` and `reactivate_prompt`; no operation selector or enabled toggle. Update edits active questions; restore a retired question with reactivate_prompt first. Retirement preserves ID/text/metadata/history, including never-scanned questions, after later roster saves. Competitors/Topic entities use `add_competitor`, `update_competitor` and `remove_competitor`. Read complete saved entries with `list_competitors` (all tracking states by default; optional tracked filter; default 25/maximum 100); follow every nextCursor with unchanged filters and restart after edits. The same add/update tools accept exact aliases, domains and ambiguousAliases as separate fields, with omission preserving and null/[] clearing a supplied group. Tracking off retains membership; remove preserves identity/history. Clear/change Topic focalEntityId before removal/untracking. After a committed-save identity error inspect the configured list and repair through update_competitor with the saved row id in competitorId; do not duplicate creation. Discovered entries use the separate paginated `list_discovered_competitors`. Model choices use `create_site`/`update_site` or `create_topic`/`update_topic` with `models: [{id, enabled}]` (IDs also enumerated in those schemas). Unspecified choices are preserved; null clears, [] is invalid. New configuration creation patches tier-aware defaults, while retries preserve saved models. Automatic schedules use `update_scan_schedule` for brand Sites only; confirm before enabling or increasing recurring allowance commitment. Frequency/enabled are partial edits; valid IANA timeZone is only a next-run calculation input, defaults to UTC, is not persisted, and cannot be the only edit. Topics remain manual-only.

   Discover selectable refs with `list_regions` using search, countryCode, kind and page. Follow page/pageCount; each page has at most 50 entries. Use `add_scan_region`/`remove_scan_region` for scan defaults. Prompt overrides take precedence; null/[] clears an override so it inherits defaults, then Global when defaults are empty. Removing the last default is valid and does not erase prompt overrides. The allowance counts distinct selected refs across defaults and enabled overrides. Unknown new refs are refused; obsolete defaults can be removed. No region edit launches a Scan.

3. For audience-specific questions, reuse the relevant persona from step 1. If the requested audience is missing, define it with `create_persona` within authorized setup work and take the returned `item.id`. Include it in `personas` in the same `create_prompt` call that supplies `text` and `category`. Include relevant configured `products` and `journeyStages` where applicable. Verify saved IDs/names in the returned prompt or `list_prompts`. Do not invent definitions or force audience-neutral questions into a persona; ask about ambiguous mappings. Topics do not require products or purchase stages.
4. Assignment classifies questions; express any required audience perspective in their text. Missing an assignment prevents filtering on that dimension or placement in its corresponding matrix cells; the question remains in the catalogue and other assignments still work. Omitted arrays on creation leave that dimension unassigned. On update/reactivation , omission preserves assignments; `[]` or null clears them. See `semantic-model.md` for an example.
5. Changes are saved to working configuration immediately; subsequent Scans use that setup. They do not rewrite earlier Scan snapshots or answers. Confirm configuration changes unless already authorized by the user's request. Show the complete setup and explain Scan allowance use. You must explicitly confirm with the user before starting the scan. Once they confirm, use `trigger_scan` to run the latest saved setup. Read a historical Scan's setup with `get_scan_config` and its `scanId`.

## Work the recommendation board

For "what should we do", "approve this", "add this to our plan", "track this activity".

1. `get_recommendations` pages the current board (default 10/max 20), ordered by impact, publication date and ID. Preserve every filter while following nextCursor. Each row includes up to 10 linked prompts; continue its promptsNextCursor through `get_recommendation` using promptCursor. `get_recommendation` returns saved stage/movement, origin, review flag, dates, grades, scope, seed and the complete brief. Evidence and prompt links page independently (default 10/max 20): follow evidenceNextCursor as evidenceCursor and promptsNextCursor as promptCursor with the same subject/recommendation. Evidence source Scan/prompt IDs identify the actual quote source, including historical same-subject evidence. baselineMeasured=false means no measured baseline; standing 0 alone does not prove AI omitted the entity. Restart pagination after edits or board changes.
2. Use the named recommendation moves with siteId/recommendationId: `approve_recommendation` or `decline_recommendation` in review; `start_recommendation_work` once approved; `record_recommendation_publication` in approved/underway when work has actually gone live and no publication date exists; `confirm_recommendation_result` only when movement is ready, readyAt exists, no confirmation exists, and the board is approved/underway. `archive_recommendation` accepts review/approved/underway; `restore_recommendation` returns archived work to review, clearing only archive date/reason and preserving approval/publication/readiness history. `acknowledge_recommendation_review` clears needsReview and changes only needs_review movement to stable; it does not approve. Each move records an actor/activity event and returns saved detail (first ten evidence entries and first ten prompt references, continued through get_recommendation). Illegal moves are refused. No move publishes website content or triggers a Scan; publication recording only records work the user has put live. After uncertain delivery/readback failure inspect `get_recommendation` before retrying: the move may already be committed. Repeated moves can fail state guards or create another event after intervening moves. All eight require recommendations:update; there is no combined action selector.
   Content create/edit keeps required title, question and whatToDo nonblank. why:null clears the optional explanation; pagesToFix:null/[] and evidence:null/[] clear their respective collections. Omitted fields survive. Only user-origin, unconfirmed and unarchived recommendations can be edited; any authorized Account writer may edit them. Seeds/evidence must belong to this Site/Topic; older scans and other same-subject narratives remain usable. Equal-value edits have no activity change. Creates/edits return bounded detail, and a write may have saved even if its readback fails. Inspect the board/detail before retrying an uncertain create; a duplicate guard within one narrative/raising Scan is not an exactly-once guarantee.

3. Read saved work with `list_recommendation_actions` and `list_recommendation_notes`, not a write. Both require siteId and recommendationId, default to 10 entries, allow at most 20, and return nextCursor; follow every non-null cursor with unchanged targets and restart after edits. Optional activityId returns one exact saved item or an empty page, without a cursor. Action reads include all saved references (URLs and free-text notes); note reads preserve complete bodies. `get_recommendation` does not supply action/note IDs.
4. Actions use `create_recommendation_action`, `update_recommendation_action` and `delete_recommendation_action`. Creation requires title/status; update uses activityId and at least one editable field. Omitted fields stay unchanged; description/date:null clears; references:null/[] clears. Titles must be nonblank, status is planned/in_progress/done, and due dates are valid YYYY-MM-DD dates. Marking an action done does not move the recommendation. Notes use `create_recommendation_note`, `update_recommendation_note` and `delete_recommendation_note`; create/update require a nonblank full body. Creates/updates return one saved action/note with stable ID and timestamps. Deletes preserve existing permanent removal of that item and return deletedActivityId; system events cannot be edited/deleted. Read the matching list after uncertainty before retrying a create, which can duplicate. Repeated deletion fails not_found. No original-author-only restriction is enforced within authorised Account access. These tools do not start a Scan or publish externally.
5. When the user spots an activity the Scan did not raise, `create_recommendation`: pick the narrative it is about (`get_narratives`), read that narrative's claims first (`get_narrative_claims`) so the brief is grounded, then write the title, the buyer question and what to do. To pin a supporting quote, pass that claim's `claimId` as the `seed` (`{kind:"claim", id: claimId}`) — otherwise the recommendation is raised with no evidence quotes. It is raised in review under the user and tracked by every later Scan.
6. To change a recommendation you raised, `edit_recommendation`: pass only the fields you want to change (title, question, whatToDo, why, pagesToFix, the grades, or `evidence` as a `claimId[]` that replaces the pinned quotes). Scan-raised recommendations cannot be edited, nor can one that is confirmed or archived; the standing and board position are unaffected.

To retire a question use `retire_prompt`; to restore its existing identity/history use `reactivate_prompt`. Both return the complete saved prompt in the same projection as list_prompts. Retirement never permanently deletes a question.

## Post-Scan triage

For "run a scan and tell me what changed".

1. Show the setup to be run and explain that it consumes Scan allowance. You must explicitly confirm with the user before starting the scan, then call `trigger_scan` after they confirm.
2. The response returns `scan.id` immediately; the Scan itself takes a while. Pass that ID as `scanId` to `get_scan_status` to follow the same Scan. It returns stage, progressPct, counts, outcome and dates; omitting scanId retains the five-recent-Scans view, and list_scans provides complete paginated history. Do not busy-poll: check when the user asks, or after a meaningful wait in a long-running session, spacing repeated checks about 15–30 seconds apart and respecting retryAfterSeconds on rate-limit errors. Stop at status completed or failed; outcome distinguishes successful completion, failure and cancellation. Percentage measures work rather than elapsed time: 100% does not prove success, and collected answers can still await analysis. Count units are individual AI requests, not saved questions; totalRequests is zero before requests are materialised.
3. When `get_scan_status` reports the Scan completed, run the visibility audit above and lead with what changed since the previous Scan.
4. If the Scan failed, report the status honestly and suggest checking with support rather than re-triggering repeatedly.

### Narrative and answer evidence

Use `get_narratives` to find saved narrative UUIDs, then `get_narrative_claims` with the chosen narrativeId and returned scanId. Both use fixed pages of twenty and no limit argument. Follow every nextCursor with unchanged subject and filters. Claim quotes, sources, passages and drivers are complete; sourceResultId identifies the actual answer for `get_prompt_result_trends`.

`get_prompt_results` returns complete answers directly, default five and maximum ten. These three reads retain the selected Scan on continuation, even if a newer Scan completes. Narrow the first request before paging. Current prompt metadata supplies classification, including retired prompts; changed membership invalidates continuation. Restart after metadata edits or evidence repairs. The returned total covers the full matching scope. Saved Topic subjectContext describes that Scan's research perspective; null means legacy context is unavailable. Neutral Topics do not acquire an invented primary-brand standing. Row counts bound records, not text bytes.

### Reference domains and exact source pages

`get_references` returns domain summaries in fixed pages of twenty with total, nextCursor and scopeToken. Each summary includes citation/reference counts, distinct prompt count, canonical platforms and complete URL/history totals; full URLs and history are retrieved through `get_reference_domain`. Follow every overview page before claiming a complete domain inventory. No caller limit is accepted.

Call `get_reference_domain` with the exact returned domain, the same filters and overview scopeToken to use the same completed-Scan boundary. A standalone call without tokens establishes its own current scope. Detail returns up to twenty full URL strings and twenty date/Scan history entries with independent totals and continuation. Pass urlNextCursor as urlCursor and trendNextCursor as trendCursor independently. Omitting a collection cursor returns its first page; following one does not advance the other. Tokens/cursors must agree. New completed Scans do not extend a pinned walk; restart after classification edits or evidence repairs.

History retains metric date, Scan ID, exact completedAt, counts and complete saved nullable Topic subjectContext. Multiple metric dates within one Scan remain separate observations. No matching domain evidence is empty-success. These reads do not fetch stored URLs or launch Scans. Page sizes bound records, not individual URL/context bytes.
